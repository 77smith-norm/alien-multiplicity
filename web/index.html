Browser Build

Alien Multiplicity

The full LiteGDK build runs in your browser. Shoot each beholder down the chain from mega to mini, and survive the crowd when every clean hit makes more enemies.

Controls
A/D or arrows move, W or Up jumps, mouse aims, Space or click fires.

Tip
Fast kills build combo score. Random hits on survivors break the chain.

Launch the browser build

If audio is locked, click once inside the launcher page to start the runtime.
